## What grows

GUM can add concept records, executable skills, confidence and outcome statistics, learned word mappings, acquisition-strategy parameters, and hash-linked experience records. That is genuine machine-state growth. It is not currently unbounded architectural self-rewrite.

## What is engineered

The outer lifecycle, available sensors and actions, clustering/search procedures, memory formats, safety boundaries, and success signals are designed by people. The particular concepts, action meanings, skill sequences, word-feature mappings, and updated acquisition weights are learned within those boundaries.

That division is normal in machine learning. The scientific question is not whether the learner was made by engineers; it is whether the tested answer was withheld and whether experience caused a reproducible, useful change.
