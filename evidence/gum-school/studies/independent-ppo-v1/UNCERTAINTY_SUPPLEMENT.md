# Supplemental uncertainty description

Registered before fixed final outcomes are available. The original primary
measure, bootstrap/t intervals and decision rule remain unchanged. As a
supplemental description, report the two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson interval for
the probability that an independent team meets the already defined rate/gain
threshold. This treats the team, not its members or episodes, as the unit.
It is not an interval for population episode completion rate and is not used
to choose a candidate or change the decision. Four failures produce a broad
interval of approximately [0, .602], illustrating why an all-zero bootstrap
does not establish impossibility or precise population reliability.
